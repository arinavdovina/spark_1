# Brand spec — S.P.A.R.K.

## Primary brand asset
- `assets/spark-poster.jpg` — supplied conference poster. The site references the real asset directly in the About section.

## Recognition cues extracted from the poster
- Midnight/navy field
- Warm ivory / champagne typography
- Thin double-line ticket border
- Circular / semicircular ticket notches
- Four-point spark/star ornaments
- Strong centered S.P.A.R.K. wordmark
- Editorial, formal event atmosphere rather than playful EdTech visuals

## Color tokens
- Midnight navy: `#070B28`
- Deep navy: `#0B1238`
- Ivory: `#FFF1D3`
- Warm paper: `#F2DDB0`
- Muted gold: `#D6AE67`
- Soft gold: `#EFD5A1`

## Typography
- Display/editorial: Cormorant Garamond
- UI/body: Manrope

## Asset policy
The original poster remains intact and is not redrawn. The website borrows its geometry and visual cues through CSS while referencing the supplied poster as an image asset.
